Privacy Policy

How Quantichy collects and handles your data.
Last updated: 3 May 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information Quantichy (“we”, “us”) collects when you use our trading analytics dashboard, how we use it, and the choices you have. We aim to keep this short and readable.

1. Information we collect

  • Account data: email address and display name from your sign-in provider (Google or email/password via Firebase Auth).
  • Usage data: trades, positions, and session history you create inside the app. Stored in your private Firestore document.
  • Wallet data: if you connect a Web3 wallet, we store the public address you authorize. We never request private keys or seed phrases.
  • Device data: standard server logs (IP address, browser type) for security and reliability.

2. How we use it

  • To run the dashboard, save your positions, and sync them across your devices.
  • To authenticate you and protect your account.
  • To improve the product — diagnose issues, measure performance, and prioritize features.

3. Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We share data only with infrastructure providers required to run the service (Firebase, Netlify) under their respective data-processing terms.

4. Your choices

  • Disconnect a connected wallet at any time from the Account page.
  • Request export or deletion of your account data by emailing support@quantichy.com.
  • Decline non-essential cookies via your browser settings.

5. Security

We use industry-standard encryption in transit (HTTPS) and at rest. Account passwords are managed by Firebase Authentication and never visible to Quantichy staff.

6. Changes to this policy

If we make material changes, we’ll update the “Last updated” date at the top and surface the change inside the app.

7. Contact

Questions? Email us at support@quantichy.com.
